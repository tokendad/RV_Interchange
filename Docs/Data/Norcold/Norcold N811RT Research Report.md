## 1. Research Summary

`N811RT` is best identified as a **Norcold refrigerator model/configuration code**, not as a replaceable service-part number.

An exact-model ManualsLib page lists a service manual for the **Norcold N811RT**. The linked manual is Norcold/Thetford Publication 619394E for the N61X/N8XX family. Multiple independent owner and repair records also use the exact `N811RT` designation.

The strongest primary documentation places the refrigerator in the **N811/N81X family**: a nominal **7.5 ft³, two-way RV absorption refrigerator** operating on either **120 V AC or LP gas**, with 12 V DC used by its electronic controls.

Norcold’s service manual says letters appended to model numbers identify factory-installed accessories. However, none of the successfully opened primary documents defines the exact meaning of `RT`. It should not be assumed to mean right-hand hinge, trim, color, or another configuration without additional documentation or label evidence.

### Wiring and controls

The service manual contains wiring pictorials and schematics for N61X/N81X refrigerators. The circuits include:

- Optical control/display
- Lower power board
- Thermistor
- AC heater
- Gas valve
- Ignition/flame-sense electrode
- Interior lamp
- Divider heater
- Optional fan circuit

The refrigerator model/serial label is located in the fresh-food compartment near the upper-right area below the divider. The cooling unit has a separate barcode and serial label. Both numbers are important because several service components change at specific production breakpoints.

### Part `630762`

An eBay listing explicitly advertises `630762` as an N811RT optical control board. However:

1. The claim comes from a marketplace seller.
2. `630762` was not found in the opened 2022 official N61/N81 parts list.
3. The official catalog identifies several different optical controls based on serial number and color.

Therefore, `630762` is an **unverified compatibility claim**. It could be an older assembly number, a bundled control, a seller-created interchange, or an incorrect listing.

## 7. Recall and Safety Findings

The current official Thetford recall page lists several Norcold campaigns. The identified model lists include certain N841, N821, N641, N621, N305/N306, older small refrigerators, and 1200-series refrigerators. **N811 and N811RT are not named in the current model lists reviewed.**

The NHTSA documentation for recall 10E-049 concerns 1200-series models and does not establish coverage for N811RT.

This absence is not proof that a particular refrigerator has never received a serial-specific bulletin, cooling-unit replacement, or field repair. Recall status should be checked using both the refrigerator serial number and cooling-unit serial number.

Official current channels:

- [Thetford recall information](https://www.thetford.com/us/recall-information/)
- [Thetford recall contact form](https://www.thetford.com/us/recall-contact-form)
- [NHTSA recall 10E-049 document](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2011/RCMN-11V074-8970.pdf)

Gas-absorption refrigerator service involves propane, combustion, 120 V AC, 12 V DC, and high-temperature components. Safety switches and lockout functions should not be bypassed. Gas-leak, burner, electrical, and cooling-unit work should follow the service manual and applicable technician procedures.

### Bottom line

> **Norcold N811RT is an N811/N81X-series, approximately 7.5 ft³, two-way gas/electric RV absorption refrigerator configuration.**

Use N811/N81X service documentation as the technical baseline. For parts or complete replacement, use:

- Refrigerator model and serial number
- Cooling-unit serial number
- Existing board and component markings
- Door swing
- Panel and trim configuration
- Rough-opening measurements
- Ventilation arrangement
- Available propane, AC, and DC services

The exact meaning of the `RT` suffix remains unresolved.
