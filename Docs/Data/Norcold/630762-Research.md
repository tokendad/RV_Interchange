## 2. Executive Finding

**Verified identity:** Norcold `630762` is a discontinued Norcold refrigerator **front optical display/control board**, commonly called an **eyebrow board**. Exact-number listings and photographs identify it as a Norcold board used in at least **some N611 and N811 refrigerators**.

The board face matches the analog two-slider optical-control assembly documented for the Norcold **N61X/N81X family**:

- Three-position `AUTO / OFF / GAS` mode selector
- Green `ON` indicator
- Amber `GAS` indicator
- Five-position temperature selector, `1` through `5`
- `5` is the coldest setting

**Important limitation:** Norcold’s current official N61/N81 parts list does **not** list `630762` as an orderable service part. Instead, it lists serial-dependent optical-control assemblies and replacement kits such as `621988`, `628979`, `637774`, and `637775`. Therefore:

> The evidence strongly supports `630762` as an OEM board-level, production, or discontinued-stock identifier, but no primary Norcold document found in this search proves a universal one-to-one supersession from `630762` to any single current or former service kit.

The safest replacement rule is to match:

1. The number on the removed board or assembly
2. Refrigerator model and suffix
3. Refrigerator serial number
4. Connector count, keying, and orientation
5. Front-panel control layout
6. Rear power-board compatibility

## 3. Historical Identity and Company Lineage

Norcold was founded in 1959 as a refrigerator manufacturer serving mobile applications. The company later established operations in Sidney, Ohio. Thetford acquired Norcold in 1997.

Thetford’s current official recall-information page states that Norcold LLC entered Chapter 11 proceedings in late 2025, that certain assets were acquired by Dave Carter & Associates, and that a liquidating trustee was appointed in February 2026. This corporate transition does not establish anything specific about part `630762`, but it explains why older Norcold technical documents continue to appear on Thetford-hosted pages while current parts availability may be fragmented.

No evidence was found that part `630762` itself is recall-specific.

### 4.2 Primary-document family identification

The exact retail photographs match the optical-control panel shown in Norcold N61X/N81X documentation.

The official N61/N81 owner’s manual documents:

- A three-position mode selector for `AUTO`, `OFF`, and `GAS`
- Green and amber indicator lights
- A five-position temperature selector
- Setting `5` as the coldest
- A requirement for 12-volt DC control power

A Norcold service manual, preserved on a dealer technical-support site, labels the assembly **“N61X/N81X Optical Control Assembly.”** It explains that the front optical board communicates with the rear power board through a foamed-in wire harness and describes removal of the front box, retaining screws, board screws, and harness connection.

An older N61X owner’s manual includes a pictorial/wiring diagram labeling the front **display board** and rear **power board**.

**Finding:** The function and product family are verified at high confidence. The exact number `630762`, however, is not printed in the primary manuals found.

### 4.3 Official parts-list conflict and supersession issue

The current official N61/N81 parts list provides the following serial-dependent optical-control assemblies and service kits:

| Application shown in official parts list | Original assembly | Service direction |

| Certain N611V/N811V applications after July 2011 / later serial range | `636105` | Use kit `637776` |
| Brown-panel variant | `629079` | Use kit `637777` |

`630762` does not appear in that official parts list.

### 4.4 Interpretation of the numbering

The most supportable interpretation is:

- `630762` is a genuine Norcold number associated with the physical front optical/eyebrow board.
- It may be a **board-level, production, vendor, or discontinued-stock identifier** rather than the complete service-assembly number used in Norcold’s parts catalog.
- The alternate marking `1172-321` appears on photographed boards and in marketplace descriptions.
- Service replacement cannot be established from `630762` alone because Norcold used serial-dependent optical assemblies and kits.

This is a **strong inference**, not a verified factory supersession statement.

### 4.5 Fitment warning

A dealer exact-number listing limits fitment to **some** N611/N811 units and instructs buyers to confirm `630762` on the removed board.

Low-priority aftermarket listings often claim that one replacement board fits all N611 and N811 models or replaces `621988`, `628979`, and `637775`. That broad claim conflicts with the serial divisions in Norcold’s official parts list and should not override the primary document.

An anecdotal JustAnswer post describes an N611 owner whose old board was marked `630762` but whose ordered `621988` replacement had a different connector. This is not primary evidence, but it reinforces the need to verify the connector and serial number.

**Recommended fitment standard:** Treat `630762` as an exact physical-board match only when the removed board carries that marking or the alternate marking is conclusively matched, and when model, serial number, connector, and control face agree.

## 5. Technical Details

### Component type

- Front optical display/control board
- Common trade names: eyebrow board, optical board, display board, control board
- Installed behind the refrigerator’s upper front control panel
- Part of the N61X/N81X control architecture

### User controls

- Mode selector: `AUTO / OFF / GAS`
- Temperature selector: five positions
- Coldness scale: `1` through `5`
- Coldest setting: `5`
- Status indicators: green `ON` LED and amber `GAS` LED
- LEDs may also be used for fault indication under service-manual procedures

### Electrical and system relationship

- Refrigerator controls require 12 VDC
- Front optical board communicates with the rear power board
- Communication passes through a built-in/foamed-in harness
- Board, harness, and rear power-board compatibility must be preserved

### Service cautions

Before removal or connector inspection:

- Turn the refrigerator off
- Isolate applicable 120 VAC and 12 VDC power
- Record connector orientation and wire routing
- Compare the old and replacement board side by side
- Do not assume that similar-looking N611/N811 boards have identical connectors

The service manual contains a text inconsistency in one section concerning the highest temperature-selector number, while the official owner’s manual and the five-position control graphic establish `1–5`, with `5` coldest.

- `630762` is documented by multiple independent sellers as a Norcold optical display/control or eyebrow board.
- Exact-board photographs match the N61X/N81X optical-control face shown in Norcold manuals.
